## Goal
A patient can set a dose by a moment in their day ("after Fajr", "before breakfast", "with Iftar") instead of a
clock time. The app works out the clock time for each day.

## Anchors and their times (Asia/Amman)
| Anchor | Time used for the dose |
|---|---|
| After Fajr / Dhuhr / Asr / Maghrib / Isha | that day's prayer time + 15 min |
| Before / with / after breakfast, lunch, dinner | the patient's meal time − 30 min / at it / + 30 min |
| With Suhoor | that day's Fajr − 45 min |
| With Iftar | that day's Maghrib |

- **Prayer times** are calculated in the app for Amman (31.95° N, 35.93° E), Jordan's method
  (Ministry of Awqaf: Fajr 18°, Isha 18°, Asr = shadow 1×). No internet service, no new dependency.
  Checked against the official Amman timetable for a few dates (aim: within 2 minutes).
- **Meal times**: Settings → "My meal times" (breakfast / lunch / dinner), defaults 08:00 / 14:00 / 20:00,
  stored with the login account like "My details" (no table).

## What the patient sees
- **Add medication, step 4 (times):** each dose row has two ways: "Clock time" (as now) or "A moment in my day".
  The second opens big buttons: the 5 prayers, the 3 meals (then before / with / after), Suhoor, Iftar.
  The row then shows e.g. "After Fajr (today 05:07)".
- **Today / reminder:** the dose shows its anchor and today's time: "After Fajr, 05:07"; the reminder title says
  "Time for your medicine: after Fajr". Doses still sit in the morning / noon / evening / night boxes by their time.
- **My medicines:** chips read "After Fajr" (coloured by part of day).
- **Doctor summary (F9) and WhatsApp text:** "after Fajr" instead of a clock time.
- **Settings:** new "My meal times" section; changing a meal time moves all doses anchored to it, from tomorrow (TIMES.md).
- Existing medicines keep their clock times. No medical advice: the app never suggests an anchor.

## Data (one database change; owner asked before applying)
`medications.anchors text[]` (nullable), one entry per dose in the same order as `times`:
empty string = clock time, or a code: `fajr`, `dhuhr`, `asr`, `maghrib`, `isha`, `suhoor`, `iftar`,
`breakfast:before|with|after`, `lunch:…`, `dinner:…`. A check keeps the codes to this list.
`times` keeps a clock time for every dose (today's time when saved) so older code still works.
Dose logs are unchanged: each day's dose is keyed by its actual scheduled moment.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Prayer times for Amman match the official timetable within 2 minutes for 3 sample dates.
2. Add medication: a dose can be set to an anchor; the review and the saved card show it.
3. "After Fajr" on a given day = Fajr + 15 min; meals ± 30 min; Suhoor = Fajr − 45; Iftar = Maghrib.
4. Today, the reminder, My medicines and the doctor summary show the anchor and the day's time.
5. Changing a meal time in Settings moves the anchored doses from tomorrow (saved with the account).
6. Medicines added before F3 still work unchanged.
7. Settings switch "Prayer and Ramadan times": off by default (only meal anchors offered); on shows prayers +
   Suhoor / Iftar; the choice is saved with the account; turning it off keeps existing anchored doses working.
8. Arabic (RTL) and English; text ≥ 18px; buttons ≥ 48px; phone and laptop.
