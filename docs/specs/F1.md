# F1 — Reminders with Taken / Snooze / Skip (approved and built 20 Nov 2026)

Owner decisions (20 Nov): chime + vibration; a new Settings tab for the demo tools; Demo speed shrinks all
waits (second reminder 15 s, snooze 15 s, Missed after 60 s).

## Goal
While the app is open, the patient is reminded when a dose is due and answers with one tap.
Web limit (CLAUDE.md): an in-app banner while the app is open; no reminders when it is closed.

## Behaviour
- **When:** a dose's time arrives (today, Asia/Amman) and it has no answer yet. Checked every few seconds.
- **Banner** at the top of Today and My medicines (not on login or the Add medication steps):
  "Time for your medicine · 08:00 · Glucophage 500 mg · 1 tablet" with three big buttons:
  **✓ Taken** · **Snooze 15 min** · **Skip**. Several doses due at once: one at a time, "1 of 3".
- **Taken / Skip:** saved in the dose log (same as the Today buttons); the banner closes.
- **Snooze:** saved as `snoozed`; the banner comes back 15 min later. Today shows "Snoozed until 08:15".
- **Follow-up:** no answer after 15 min → the banner changes to a stronger "Second reminder" (red edge)
  and alerts again. After 60 min with no answer the dose is "Missed" (F2 rule) and the banner goes away.
- **Alert:** a short chime + vibration (on phones that allow it) when a reminder or follow-up appears;
  the browser tab title shows "(1) Med Friend". (Browsers allow sound only after the first tap in the app.)

## Settings tab (new, third bottom tab)
- **Demo speed** switch (per device, saved in localStorage): minutes become seconds
  (follow-up 15 s, snooze 15 s, missed after 60 s), so the whole flow is visible in a minute.
- **Trigger next reminder now** button: shows the banner at once for the next dose that has no answer
  (it is logged against that dose's real time).
- Also moved here: Log out, "Logged in as". (The language switch stays at the top of every screen.) One-line disclaimer:
  "Med Friend only records what you enter. It does not give medical advice."

## Acceptance criteria
1. At a dose's time, the banner appears with name, strength, amount and time, without reloading.
2. Taken / Skip save the dose (Today updates) and close the banner.
3. Snooze hides it; it comes back after 15 min (15 s with Demo speed); Today shows "Snoozed".
4. No answer for 15 min → "Second reminder"; after 60 min the banner goes and the dose is Missed.
5. Several due doses: one at a time with "1 of n".
6. Settings: Demo speed on/off is remembered on this device; "Trigger next reminder now" shows the banner at once.
7. Settings shows Log out, the account email and the disclaimer.
8. Works in Arabic; text ≥ 18px; buttons ≥ 48px.
