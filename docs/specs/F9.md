# F9 — Summary for my doctor (+ "My details" profile) (approved and built 22 Nov 2026)

Owner decisions (22 Nov): summary in the app's current language; button on the Progress tab;
patient name is essential → a small profile ("My details") in Settings with **name + year of birth**;
if no name yet, the summary asks for it right on the page (and saves it to the profile).

## Goal
In one tap the patient shows a doctor or pharmacist a clean one-page record: who they are, what they take,
and how regularly they took it. Printable (browser Print → PDF) and shareable as a WhatsApp text.

## 1. "My details" in Settings
- New first section in Settings: **My details** — "Your name" (text) and "Year of birth" (4 digits, 1900 to this year,
  optional), one **Save** button, then "Saved" message.
- Stored with the login account (Supabase Auth user metadata: `full_name`, `birth_year`). No new table, no database
  change; works on every device the patient logs in from. Only the patient can read or change it.
- Sign-up stays email + password only.
- Demo account: "Demo patient" / "مريض تجريبي", born 1968; `scripts/seed-demo.mjs` sets it again on reset.

## 2. The summary page
Opened from **Progress → "Summary for my doctor"**; a full screen with **Back**. Language = the app's language.
1. **Header:** "Medication summary", patient name, year of birth (age), today's date (Amman).
   No name yet → a "Your name" + "Year of birth" box with Save on the page itself (saved to My details).
2. **My medicines:** one row each — name + strength, amount and how often, times
   (e.g. "Glucophage 500 mg — 1 tablet, twice a day — 08:00, 20:00").
3. **How regularly I took them:** overall last 7 and last 30 days ("141 of 206 doses, 68%"),
   and the 30-day % for each medicine.
4. Footer: "Recorded by the patient in Med Friend. Not medical advice."
Buttons (not printed): **Print / Save as PDF** and **Send on WhatsApp**.

## 3. Print / Save as PDF
Browser print window. A print layout hides the buttons, tab bar and phone frame; black on white; fits one A4 page
for up to ~10 medicines.

## 4. Send on WhatsApp
Opens WhatsApp (phone app or WhatsApp Web) with the summary as ready-written text (standard `wa.me` share link).
The patient chooses the contact and presses Send in WhatsApp. Nothing is sent by the app itself.

## Not included
No medical advice or judgement of the numbers; no doctor accounts; no saved PDFs; no new dependency.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Settings → My details: name and year of birth save, survive a refresh and a new login; bad year shows a clear message.
2. Progress → "Summary for my doctor" opens the summary with name, age/year, date, every medicine, 7- and 30-day figures
   and per-medicine %; Back returns to Progress.
3. No name yet: the summary asks for it; saving shows it on the summary and in Settings.
4. Print / Save as PDF opens the print window; the printed page has no buttons, tabs or frame.
5. Send on WhatsApp opens a `wa.me` link whose text contains the name, the medicines and the adherence figures.
6. Works in Arabic (RTL) and English; text ≥ 18px on screen; buttons ≥ 48px; phone and laptop.
7. Demo account shows "Demo patient" after the seed script runs.
