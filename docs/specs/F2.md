# F2 — "Today" view (approved and built 20 Nov 2026)

Owner decisions (20 Nov): Taken / Skip buttons on each dose now (with Undo); bottom tabs Today | My medicines
(app opens on Today; Log out moved to My medicines); "Missed" 60 min after the dose time.

## Goal
The patient opens the app and sees, at a glance, every dose for today and what is still to do.

## Screen
Title "Today", today's date (Asia/Amman), and a short line: "3 of 6 doses taken".
Doses grouped by time of day, each group only if it has doses:

| Group | Times |
|---|---|
| Morning · صباحاً | 04:00–11:59 |
| Noon · ظهراً | 12:00–16:59 |
| Evening · مساءً | 17:00–20:59 |
| Night · ليلاً | 21:00–03:59 |

Each dose row (big, one per line): time · medicine name + strength · amount ("08:00 · Glucophage 500 mg · 1 tablet")
and its status:
- **Upcoming** (later today), **Due now** (from its time until 60 min after),
- **Taken 08:05** / **Skipped** (from the dose log),
- **Missed** (no answer 60 min after its time). Shown only, not stored; F8 counts it the same way.

Doses before the medicine was added (first day only) are shown without "Missed".
Empty state: "No doses today. Add a medicine to see your doses." with the Add medication button.
The view refreshes its statuses every minute while open (Due now → Missed without reloading).

## Data
Reads `medications` (times) and today's rows from `dose_logs`. Taken / Skip write a `dose_logs` row
(`scheduled_at` = today's date + the dose time in Asia/Amman; `status`; `recorded_at` = now).
One row per dose (`unique (medication_id, scheduled_at)` already in the database); Undo deletes it.

## Files (planned)
`src/screens/Today/Today.tsx`, `src/storage/doseLogs.ts`, `src/lib/time.ts` (Amman date/time helpers,
time-of-day groups), bottom tab bar component, new keys in `en.json` + `ar.json`. No new dependencies. Estimate ~3 h.

## Acceptance criteria (draft)
1. After login, the app opens on Today with today's date.
2. A medicine taken 3 times a day shows 3 doses in Morning / Noon / Evening.
3. Doses are in time order inside each group; empty groups are hidden.
4. Statuses: Upcoming, Due now, Missed follow the clock (tested with a fixed fake time).
5. Taken / Skip save to the dose log and survive a refresh; Undo removes it.
6. "x of y doses taken" counts correctly.
8. A new medicine shows on Today at once; deleting it removes its doses; empty Today offers Add medication.
9. Saving a dose offline shows a clear message.
7. Works in Arabic; text ≥ 18px; buttons ≥ 48px; phone and laptop.

Tests: `tests/F2.spec.ts` (fake clock: Wed 18 Nov 2026 13:30 Amman).
Known limit: Night doses after midnight (00:00–03:59) are listed at the end, in the Night group.
