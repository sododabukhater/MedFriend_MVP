## Goal
The phone says the dose out loud, so a patient who struggles with small text, or is across the room, still hears
which medicine to take. The app only reads what the patient entered (no medical advice).

## What the patient hears
- Arabic: «حان وقت دوائك: كونكور 5 milligram، حبة واحدة، بعد صلاة الفجر.»
- English: "Time for your medicine: Concor 5 mg, 1 tablet, after Fajr."
- Follow-up reminder (15 minutes later, no answer): starts with «تذكير: لم تُسجّل هذه الجرعة بعد.» /
  "Reminder: this dose is not recorded yet."
- The words come from the same text the screen shows (name, strength, amount, moment of the day). If the dose has a
  clock time and no moment, the time is not read (it is "now").

## What the patient sees
- **Reminder panel:** a 🔊 "Read aloud" button (large, ≥ 48 px) under the medicine name. The reminder is read by
  itself once when it appears (after the chime), and again on each tap.
- **Today top card:** the same 🔊 "Read aloud" button for the dose shown there (due now, snoozed or next).
- **Settings:** a new group "Reminders" with a switch **"Read reminders aloud"** (on by default, saved on this
  device like Demo speed). Off = no automatic voice; the 🔊 buttons still work.
- **No voice for this language on the device:** tapping 🔊 shows "Voice not available on this device." and the
  automatic voice stays silent (the chime and the panel still work).

## How it works
- The browser's own speech (`speechSynthesis`); no recording, no online service, no new dependency, no database
  change. Picks a voice for the app language (`ar…` or `en…`), preferring a local one.
- Browser rule (same as the chime): automatic speech works only after the patient has tapped the app once since
  opening it. The 🔊 button always works because it is a tap.
- Starting a new readout stops the previous one. Answering the reminder stops the voice.

## Known limits
- Voice quality depends on the device. Android and iPhone normally have an Arabic voice; laptops vary (Windows
  needs an Arabic speech pack, otherwise "Voice not available").
- A medicine name typed in Latin letters ("Concor") may sound odd in the Arabic voice. Names picked from the
  suggestion list while the app is in Arabic are saved in Arabic and read well.

## Acceptance criteria (Playwright, headless, phone + laptop; speech is replaced by a recorder in tests)
1. When a reminder appears, the reminder text is spoken once, in English with an English voice (app in English).
2. In Arabic, the spoken text is Arabic and uses an Arabic voice; anchored doses include the moment ("بعد صلاة الفجر").
3. 🔊 in the reminder reads it again; 🔊 on the Today top card reads that dose.
4. The follow-up reminder is spoken with the follow-up words.
5. Settings → "Read reminders aloud" off: no automatic speech; 🔊 still works; the setting stays after reload.
6. No voice for the language: 🔊 shows "Voice not available on this device."; nothing crashes.
7. Answering the reminder stops the voice.
8. New text in English and Arabic; buttons ≥ 48 px; RTL correct.
