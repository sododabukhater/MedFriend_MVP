## Goal
The patient photographs their own pill or box. The photo shows with that medicine, so at reminder time they see
*which* pill to take, not only its name. The app never identifies or checks the pill (no medical advice).

## What the patient sees
- **My medicines:** each card has a photo area. No photo: a dashed box "Add a photo" (camera icon). With a photo:
  the photo (about 96 px), plus "Change photo" and "Remove photo" (remove asks to confirm).
- **Add medication:** after step 4 (times), an optional **step 5 "Take a photo of your pill or box"** with
  "Take or choose a photo" and "Skip for now". A preview shows before saving. The review screen shows the photo.
  The step count becomes "Step x of 5".
- **Reminder panel:** the photo shown large (about 160 px) above the medicine name, when there is one.
- **Today:** a small round thumbnail (48 px) at the start of each dose row that has a photo.
- **Doctor summary (F9):** unchanged (no photos).
- On a phone the button offers the camera or the gallery (the phone's own choice); on a laptop, a file picker.
- While uploading: "Saving photo…"; on failure: "The photo could not be saved. Try again."

## How it works
- The browser shrinks the photo before upload (longest side 800 px, JPEG ~80 %), so a 5 MB camera photo becomes
  about 100 KB. Done with a canvas; no new dependency. Only image files are accepted.
- Stored in a **private** Supabase Storage bucket `pill-photos`, path `<user id>/<medicine id>-<timestamp>.jpg`.
  The app shows photos through short-lived signed links (1 day), refreshed when the medicine list loads.
- Changing a photo uploads the new one, then deletes the old file. Deleting a medicine, or "Reset all data",
  also deletes its photos.

## Data (storage + one column; owner approves before applying)
- Migration `20261122150000_pill_photos.sql`:
  - `medications.photo_path text` (nullable).
  - Bucket `pill-photos`: private, max 2 MB per file, images only (`image/jpeg`, `image/png`, `image/webp`).
  - RLS policies on `storage.objects` for that bucket (select / insert / update / delete): only when the first
    folder of the path is the user's own id (`(storage.foldername(name))[1] = auth.uid()::text`).
- Demo account: no photos at first (the professor can add one). `scripts/seed-demo.mjs` clears demo photos.

## Acceptance criteria (Playwright, headless, phone + laptop)
1. My medicines: "Add a photo" on a medicine → choose an image → the photo shows on the card, and stays after reload.
2. "Change photo" replaces it; "Remove photo" → confirm → the "Add a photo" box returns.
3. Add medication: step 5 can be skipped (medicine saved without photo) or used (photo shows on review and on the card).
4. The reminder panel shows the medicine's photo; the Today row shows the thumbnail.
5. Privacy: a second account cannot read or list the first account's photo (storage refuses).
6. A large image is shrunk before upload (uploaded file ≤ 800 px on the long side).
7. A non-image file is refused with a friendly message.
8. Deleting the medicine deletes its photo file.
9. Arabic and English text for every new string; RTL layout correct.
