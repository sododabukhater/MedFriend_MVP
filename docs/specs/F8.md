# F8 — Dose log + adherence % (approved and built 20 Nov 2026)

Owner decisions (20 Nov): new Progress tab; Skipped counts as not taken; demo account gets 30 days of history.

## Goal
Show the patient (and the professor) how many doses were taken, per medicine, for the last 7 and 30 days,
and the timestamped record behind it. This is the alpha's outcome measure (CLAUDE.md outcome 1).

## How adherence is counted
- A **dose that counts** = a scheduled dose in the period that is already decided:
  answered (Taken / Skipped), or Missed (no answer 60 min after its time, same rule as Today).
  Doses still Upcoming or Due now don't count yet. Doses before the medicine was added never count.
- **Adherence % = Taken ÷ doses that count**, rounded. Periods: last 7 days and last 30 days, including today.
- **On time** (secondary line): Taken within 60 min of the dose time ("12 of 14 on time").
- Fewer than 1 counted dose: show "—" and "Not enough data yet" instead of a %.
- A deleted medicine takes its history with it (known limit for the alpha).

## Screen (Progress tab, between My medicines and Settings)
1. **Overall**: two big numbers, "Last 7 days 86%" and "Last 30 days 79%", with "24 of 28 doses taken".
2. **Per medicine**: one card each: name + strength, 7-day % and 30-day % with a simple bar, "on time" line.
3. **Dose log**: newest first, grouped by day ("Thursday 19 November"), each line:
   "08:00 Glucophage 500 mg · Taken at 08:12" / "Skipped" / "Missed". Shows the last 7 days,
   with a "Show 30 days" button.
Colours: neutral (no red / green judgement); the app never comments on the numbers (no medical advice).

## Demo data
The demo account gets 30 days of past history (about 80% taken, a few late, skipped and missed doses),
with its medicines back-dated 30 days, so the professor sees real numbers. Done through `scripts/seed-demo.mjs`
(normal login, no secret key). Marked as demo by the account itself (demo@medfriend.jo).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Progress shows overall 7-day and 30-day adherence with "x of y doses taken".
2. Each medicine shows its own 7-day and 30-day % and "on time".
3. Counting follows the rules above (tested with a fixed fake date and known history).
4. The dose log lists each counted dose with its time and status, newest first, grouped by day; "Show 30 days" works.
5. A brand-new account shows "Not enough data yet" instead of a %.
6. Taking a dose on Today updates Progress.
7. Demo account shows 30 days of history.
8. Works in Arabic; text ≥ 18px; buttons ≥ 48px.

Tests: `tests/F8.spec.ts` (fake clock: Wed 18 Nov 2026 13:30 Amman, known history).
Demo history: `node scripts/seed-demo.mjs` (fixed random numbers, ~80% taken; today left unanswered).
