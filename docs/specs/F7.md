# F7 — Stock count + low-stock refill alert (approved and built 22 Nov 2026)

Owner decisions (22 Nov): the **box (pack) is defined when the medicine is added** ("the box contains 100 tablets");
the app **calculates what is left by itself** from the doses the patient marks as taken (100-tablet box, 2 tablets a
day, 10 taken after 5 days → 90 left). Warn at **7 days or less left**. The warning shows on **Today and on the
medicine card**.

## Goal
The patient never runs out by surprise: the app knows how much is left in the box and says "time to get a refill"
a week before it runs out. It only counts what the patient records; it never suggests doses (no medical advice).

## What the patient sees
- **Add medication** gets a new **step 5 "The box"** (the photo becomes step 6, then the review):
  - "How many are in one box?" with the unit next to the box (follows the form: tablets, capsules, ml, puffs,
    drops, units…). A number box; Arabic digits accepted.
  - "How many more full boxes do you have?" − / + (0 to 20, starts at 0). Added 22 Nov (owner): a patient with
    several boxes enters them here.
  - "How many are left in the box you are using?" Starts equal to the box size (a new, full box); the patient can
    lower it if the box is already open. Cannot be more than the box size.
  - With extra boxes the total shows: "In total: 78 tablets" (2 × 30 + 18).
  - While both boxes are empty the main button says **Skip for now** (for a cream or anything hard to count). A skipped medicine simply has no stock count.
  - The review screen shows "Box: 30 tablets a box · 78 left".
- **My medicines card**: a stock line under the dose line:
  - "**90 tablets left · about 45 days**".
  - 7 days or less: a yellow **Low** badge before the same line ("Low  12 tablets left · about 6 days").
  - 0 left: a red **Out of stock** badge and "None left".
  - A button **I got a new box**: adds one full box to what is left (90 + 100 = 190) after a short confirm
    ("Add one box of 100 tablets?"). A button **Correct the count** sets the number left by hand (if a pill was lost).
  - Medicines without a box (old ones, or skipped): **Set the box size** opens the same three questions.
  - **Correct the count** asks the box size and "How many do you have now in total?" (can be more than one box).
- **Today**: under the top card, one yellow card per low medicine:
  "**Glucophage: only about 6 days left.** Time to get a refill." (out of stock: "Glucophage has run out.").
  Tapping it opens My medicines.
- **Reminder panel** (added 22 Nov, owner): the reminder of a low medicine shows a yellow line under the amount:
  "Only about 4 days left. Time to get a refill." (out: "None left. Time to get a refill."). The panel closes when
  the dose is answered, so the line shows while the reminder is open.
- **Counting**: every dose marked **I took it** takes its amount off (2 tablets → −2). **Undo** puts it back
  automatically. Skipped and missed doses do not count.
- Days left = what is left ÷ (amount × doses per day), rounded down.

## How it works
- Nothing is subtracted in the database. The app stores the count at one moment and works out the rest from the
  dose log: **left = count at that moment − (amount × taken doses recorded after that moment)**. So Undo, a second
  device and Reset all data all stay correct without extra code.
- "I got a new box" and "Correct the count" save a new count and a new moment.
- The demo account (`scripts/seed-demo.mjs`) gets boxes for all six medicines, with **one of them low** (about
  5 days left) so the professor sees the warning straight away.

## Data (owner approves before applying)
Migration `2026112310xxxx_stock.sql`, three new optional columns on `medications` (no new table):
- `pack_size numeric` (> 0, ≤ 9999): how many units in one box.
- `stock_count numeric` (≥ 0, ≤ 99999): how many were left at `stock_set_at`.
- `stock_set_at timestamptz`: when that count was saved.
All three empty = no stock count. Existing RLS (owner-only) and grants already cover them.

## Acceptance criteria (Playwright, headless, phone + laptop)
1. Add medication shows step 5 "The box"; box 100 / left 100 → review shows it; the card shows "100 tablets left ·
   about 50 days" (2 a day).
2. "Left in the box in use" cannot be more than the box size; Skip for now saves the medicine without a stock count.
2c. The reminder panel of a low / out-of-stock medicine shows the refill line; a well-stocked one does not.
2b. Several boxes: 30 a box + 2 full boxes + 18 left = 78 in total (review and card).
3. Mark a dose (2 tablets) as taken → the card shows 98; Undo → 100 again; Skip does not change it.
4. 7 days or less left → yellow Low badge on the card and a yellow card on Today; tapping it opens My medicines.
5. 0 left → Out of stock (card and Today).
6. I got a new box → adds one box; Correct the count → sets the number; both stay after reload.
7. A medicine without a box shows "Set the box size" and can be given one.
8. The unit follows the form (ml for syrup, puffs for an inhaler).
9. Arabic: the new text shows in Arabic, right to left.

## Out of scope
Ordering from a pharmacy, expiry dates, push alerts when the app is closed, several boxes with different sizes.
